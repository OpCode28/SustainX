## 2. 60-25-10-5 Visual Distribution Rule for Slides

- **60% Solid Clean White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Slide backgrounds, reading space, card interiors. Keeps the slides crisp, corporate, and uncluttered.
- **25% MEIL Deep Navy (`#313490`)**: Title bars, full-bleed hero slides, major section division panels, executive summary callout boxes.
- **10% MEIL Vibrant Red (`#E91029`)**: Slide section numbers (`01.`, `02.`), active indicator arrows (`→`), audit alerts, progress bar milestones.
- **5% Supporting Neutrals (`#E8E8E8`, `#C6CBE6`, `#FFF7F9`)**: Metric chip containers, grid borders, and subtle table striping.

### Slide 08: The End-to-End ESG Workflow (6-Step Guided Pipeline)
- **Layout Pattern**: Horizontal 6-Stage Process Chevron or pill sequence (`01` through `06`) with Red numbers.
- **Step 01 - Site Data Entry**: Standardized input forms with predefined engineering units (L, kWh, MT, Hours).
- **Step 02 - Auto-Validation**: Range checks, unit conversions, and threshold anomaly alerts.
- **Step 03 - Evidence Attachment**: Mandatory upload of utility bills, weighbridge slips, and calibration certificates.
- **Step 04 - Hierarchical Review**: 2-level maker-checker approval (Project Manager $\rightarrow$ BU Head).
- **Step 05 - Automated Rollup**: Group-wide formula execution and emission factor application.
- **Step 06 - Report Generation**: Instant export of SEBI Annexure I XML/PDF and Executive Board deck.
